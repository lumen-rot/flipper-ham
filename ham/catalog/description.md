# HAM - Human Activity Mimic

HAM combines typing bursts, mouse movements, occasional clicks, and pauses over USB.

- Adjust activity from 0 to 100 percent.
- Choose 5, 10, or 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, or an unlimited session.
- Start and pause with OK. Stop and exit with Back.
- An animated ham icon indicates that a session is running.
- Customize text, typing speed, mouse range, and click frequency through the app's settings files.

## Getting started

Open a blank text document on your computer and place the pointer inside its text area. HAM opens paused. Use Up and Down to select Activity or Time, and Left and Right to adjust the selected setting.

Tested on macOS with a US QWERTY keyboard layout. No companion software is required. The app sends relative mouse movement and cannot see the screen or guarantee that clicks remain inside a window. Pointer acceleration affects the actual movement distance.

Exit HAM before connecting through qFlipper: the app uses the Flipper's USB connection as a keyboard and mouse while open.
